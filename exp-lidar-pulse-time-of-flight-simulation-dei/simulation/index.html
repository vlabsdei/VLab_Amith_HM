Mount the LiDAR Head

The rotating LiDAR sits at the centre of the room on its tripod. As it spins, it fires a laser pulse at every angular step, timing the return of each pulse to calculate distance using d = (c × t_return) / 2.

Mounting positionRoom centre, fixed
Rotation axisVertical (yaw)
Speed of light c3 × 108 m/s
Sweep typeFull 360°
