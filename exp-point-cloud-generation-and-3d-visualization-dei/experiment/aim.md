## Aim
 
Our goal here is to bring a 2D depth map to life by simulating its back-projection into a 3D point cloud! We'll explore how different factors—like scan resolution, depth range, and sensor noise—shape the density, accuracy, and overall look of the point cloud. By tweaking these settings interactively, you'll get a hands-on feel for how a 3D point cloud is structured and what makes it tick.
