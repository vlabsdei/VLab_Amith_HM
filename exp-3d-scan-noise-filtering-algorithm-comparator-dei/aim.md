## Aim
 
Our goal in this experiment is to explore how we clean up messy 3D scan data! You will get hands-on experience simulating and comparing three industry-standard noise filtering algorithms: Statistical Outlier Removal (SOR), Radius Outlier Removal (ROR), and Moving Least Squares smoothing (MLS). By the end of this lab, you'll understand how to tune these algorithms to strike the perfect balance between aggressively scrubbing away noise and carefully preserving the genuine, fine details of a 3D surface.
