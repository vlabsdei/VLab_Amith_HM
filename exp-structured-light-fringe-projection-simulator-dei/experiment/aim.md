## Aim
 
Welcome to the fascinating world of structured light 3D scanning! In this experiment, your goal is to master the **Fringe Projection Simulator**. You'll step into the shoes of a 3D vision engineer to explore how projecting simple black-and-white wavy lines (sinusoidal fringes) onto an object can reveal its exact 3D shape.

By the end of this lab, you won't just understand the math-you'll have an intuitive feel for it. You will experiment hands-on to see exactly what happens to your 3D scans when you tweak the number of projected images, adjust the thickness of the fringes, battle against annoying ambient light, or change the physical angle between your camera and projector. Get ready to turn abstract patterns into stunning 3D height maps!
