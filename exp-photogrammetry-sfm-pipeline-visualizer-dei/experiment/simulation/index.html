STEP 2 OF 5

Place the Subject

The scan subject sits on the turntable at the scene centre. The scale reference marker rests beside it, visible in every planned photograph.

Observe the 3D view - the subject and reference marker are now centred on the turntable platform.
Why a Scale Reference?

Photographs alone carry scale ambiguity. A marker of known physical size (100 mm) fixes the unit of the reconstruction, making measurements meaningful.
