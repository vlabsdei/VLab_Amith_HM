Setup Instructions

Zhang's Method Calibration:

  • Mount the tripod, camera, and the checkerboard rail system.
  • Capture the checkerboard at various distances and orientations.
  • A minimum of 5 poses is required to calculate intrinsic parameters.
  • Between 15 and 30 poses are recommended for high accuracy.
